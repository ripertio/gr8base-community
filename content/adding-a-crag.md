# How to add a Crag - Guide

## Creating the entry

1. Log in and choose **Add a crag**.
2. Fill in the name and location.
3. Save, then add photos.

## Review

When you choose **Submit for review**, the crag is shown with a "Pending review" label until our reviewers have looked at it. You get an email when it is published. If the reviewers need something changed, the crag is returned to you with a reason (also shown on the crag page and in **My crags**) and you get an email as well. Edit the crag and submit it again.
