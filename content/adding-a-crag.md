# Adding a crag

A short plain-language introduction: what this page covers and who it is for.

## Before you start

- Have the GPS location ready.
- Take at least one photo of the wall.

## Creating the entry

1. Log in and choose **Add a crag**.
2. Fill in the name and location.
3. Save, then add photos.

## Fields

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Name | The official crag name |
| Area | The valley or region |
