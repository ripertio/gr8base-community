# How do I add a crag?

## Creating the entry

1. Log in and choose **Add a crag**.
2. Fill in the name and the area. Everything else is optional, but the more you add, the more useful the entry is: approach, base area, parking, family notes and photos.
3. Save as a draft, or save and add photos.

Photos are resized and converted automatically (HEIC from iPhone works). A crag can have up to 8 photos. Pictures of the base area and the approach help most, because they show other families what to expect.

### Coordinates from your photos

If you leave **Crag coordinates** empty and upload a photo taken at the crag, the coordinates are filled in from the photo's location after saving. The first photo with a location is used; photos in the category "Parking" are skipped. Coordinates you entered yourself are never replaced.

- The photo's position is where you stood, not the exact crag. Please check it on the map and correct it if needed.
- On iPhone, the location can be removed when you pick photos from your library. Tap **Options** in the photo picker and turn on **Location**.
- The photo itself is stored without its location.

## Review

When you choose **Submit for review**, the crag is shown with a "Pending review" label until our reviewers have looked at it. You get an email when it is published. If the reviewers need something changed, the crag is returned to you with a reason (also shown on the crag page and in **My crags**) and you get an email as well. You can answer the reviewers in a message thread on the crag. Edit the crag and submit it again.

## Family rating

Rate the crag for each age group you know: babies (0–1), ages 2–4 and ages 5 and older, from 1 to 5. Leave a group empty if you do not know it.

The crag page shows your rating as the author's rating. Other logged-in users can add their own, and the average is shown next to yours as the community rating. Nobody can add a community rating to their own crag.

## Later

Other users can comment, rate and suggest changes on your crag. You get notified, and you decide which notifications you want under **Notifications → Settings**. See [How do I report something wrong on a crag?](/docs/how-do-i-report-something-wrong-on-a-crag/).
