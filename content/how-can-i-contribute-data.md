# How can I contribute?

gr8base grows through the experience of its community. You do not need to be an expert: if you have been to a crag with your family, you know something others want to know.

For most of the ways to contribute you need an account. Browsing and reading is open to everyone.

## Quick ways to help


- **Rate a crag** for babies, ages 2–4 or ages 5+, and **comment** on it. This takes a minute.
- **Add photos** of the base area, the approach and the parking. A few pictures say more than a long description.
- **Suggest a change** if something is wrong or out of date: [How do I report something wrong on a crag?](/docs/how-do-i-report-something-wrong-on-a-crag/)

## Add a crag

Know a crag that is missing? Add it. Only the name and the area are required, you can fill in the rest later. Reviewers check every crag before it is published. Step by step: [How do I add a crag?](/docs/adding-a-crag/)

## Help with the project

- Report a bug or suggest an improvement: [How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?](/docs/how-do-i-report-a-bug-or-suggest-an-improvement/)
- The code is open source: [Where can I find the code?](/docs/where-is-the-code/)
