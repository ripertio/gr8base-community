# How do I suggest changes on a crag?

If the parking changed, the approach is closed or a rating does not fit any more, use **Suggest a change** on the crag page.

## Sending a suggestion

1. Log in and open the crag. (Not logged in? The crag page has a link that takes you to the form after logging in.)
2. Choose **Suggest a change**.
3. Pick what it is about: location, approach, parking, base, family rating, photos or other.
4. Describe what should change. Photos are optional, up to 8 at a time.
5. Choose **Send suggestion**.

You can suggest changes to published crags made by other people. For your own crags, edit them directly.

## Who sees it?

Only you, the crag's creator and the moderators. Your suggestion is not shown on the crag page for everyone else, and your photos are only published if they are accepted. Location data is removed from photos.

## What happens next?

- The creator and the moderators get a notification.
- You can talk about it in a message thread on the suggestion. Anyone in the thread gets notified of new messages.
- The creator or a moderator edits the crag and closes the suggestion as **done** or **declined**, usually with a short note. You get a notification either way.
- If they accept your photos, they are added to the crag's gallery. All other photos of the suggestion are deleted when it is closed.
- Changed your mind? You can **withdraw** an open suggestion. Its photos are deleted.

You find your suggestions on the crag page. Notification types can be switched on and off under **Notifications → Settings**.

## Something else is wrong?

If it is not about a single crag, for example the website itself, see [How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?](/docs/how-do-i-report-a-bug-or-suggest-an-improvement/).
