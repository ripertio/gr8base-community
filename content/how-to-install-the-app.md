# How do I install gr8base on my phone?

gr8base is a website that you can put on your home screen. It then opens like an app, without the browser bar, and has its own icon. There is nothing to download from an app store.

## iPhone and iPad

Use **Safari**. Other browsers on iPhone cannot add websites to the home screen.

1. Open [gr8base.com](https://gr8base.com).
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

## Android

Use **Chrome**.

1. Open [gr8base.com](https://gr8base.com).
2. Tap the **menu** (three dots).
3. Choose **Install app** (on some phones: **Add to Home screen**).
4. Confirm.

## Notifications

Once the app is installed, open it from your home screen and go to **Notifications → Settings** to turn on notifications for this device. On iPhone and iPad this only works from the home screen app, not from Safari.

## Removing it

Remove the icon like any other app. Your account and your crags stay on gr8base.
