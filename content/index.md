# Frequently asked questions

On sport climbing you spend most of the day at the base, not on the rock. So why not decide which crag to visit by the base, where your needs are met? For example, you know your loved ones can play and are safe. 

Gr8base is a community-reviewed collection of climbing crags, from the point of view of that base not just where you climb. It rates every crag for babies, children aged 2-4 and children aged 5 and older, and shows photos so you can judge the conditions before you go. It is not a guidebook.
