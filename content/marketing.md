# Marketing
Both measures will only start once the platform reaches a minimum of 200 crags.

## Incentives for the community

To motivate the community to add more crags, we will approach climbing guidebook publishers. Contributors who publish the most crags will be able to choose one guidebook per year from our partners.

## Covering costs

To cover running costs (server, domain, development), we will approach climbing hardware manufacturers and ask about advertising opportunities.
