# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data gr8base community processes, why, and what rights you have. It applies to the website and to the services behind it.

## 1. Overview

We collect as little data as possible. There is no third-party tracking. Data is processed only to run accounts, store crag entries and photos, keep the site secure, and some fun analytics like how many views per day without any logging on who is visiting. Our rules for submitted content are in the [Terms of Use](/docs/terms/).

## 2. Data we process

**Account data.** When you sign up: username, email address, and a password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (Argon2), never in plain text. Whether you verify your email is optional and controlled by the site configuration.

**Content you submit.** Crag entries (name, location coordinates, description, parking and family notes, guidebook references), uploaded photos with captions, and the username that created them. Uploaded photos are re-encoded on upload, which removes embedded metadata such as location or device information (EXIF). Photos and captions may show or name people. Please do not upload identifiable people without their consent.

**Session data.** After you log in, a session cookie keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the login function. We do not use marketing or tracking cookies.

**Server logs.** Our hosting and network infrastructure temporarily records technical data such as IP address, time of request, requested page, and browser type. This is needed to operate and secure the site.

**Emails.** We send account-related emails (for example sign-up confirmation or password reset) through the transactional email provider Resend.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Creating and operating your account | Account data, session data | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Publishing and managing crag entries and photos | Content, username | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Sending account emails | Email address | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Operating and securing the server | Server logs | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable and secure service) |
| Complying with legal obligations | As required | Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR |

## 4. Service providers and recipients

We use the following processors. Data is passed on only to the extent needed for their service.

- **Hosting:** The server is a homeserver.
- **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Tunnel): routes traffic to the server and may process connection data as a proxy.
- **Resend** (transactional email): processes email addresses and message content for delivery.

Where a provider processes data outside the EU/EEA, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

**External links.** Some pages link to Google Maps. Google receives data only when you actively click the link, and the Google privacy policy applies from that point on.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Retention

- Account data: until you delete your account or request deletion.
- Crag entries and photos: as long as they remain published. When you delete your account, your username is removed from the entries and photos you created. Crag information that others rely on (such as location and approach details) stays published without any link to you. Personal data in them, such as photos showing people or captions naming people, is removed on request. See section 5 of our [Terms of Use](/docs/terms/).
- Server logs: only for as long as needed for operation and security, then deleted.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your personal data (Art. 15)
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
- have data deleted (Art. 17)
- restrict processing (Art. 18)
- data portability (Art. 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, email impressum@gr8base.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For Jena, Germany, this is the Thüringer Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (TLDI), Häßlerstraße 8, 99096 Erfurt, [tlfdi.de](https://www.tlfdi.de).

## 7. Children

The site is intended for adults who manage their own accounts. Children may view public crag information, but should not create accounts or upload personal data without a parent or guardian.

## 8. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, CSRF protection, and regular updates of the software we run. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We may update this policy when the site changes. The current version is always available on this page.
