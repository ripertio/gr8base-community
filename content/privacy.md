This policy explains what personal data gr8base community processes, why, and what rights you have. It applies to the website and to the services behind it.

## 1. Overview

We collect as little data as possible. There is no third-party tracking. Data is processed only to run accounts, store crag entries and photos, keep the site secure, and some fun analytics like how many views per day without any logging on who is visiting. Our rules for submitted content are in the [Terms of Use](/docs/terms/).

## 2. Data we process

**Account data.** When you sign up: username, email address, and a password. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (Argon2), never in plain text. Whether you verify your email is optional and controlled by the site configuration.

**Profile data.** Optionally a short bio and an avatar image. Avatars are re-encoded and resized on upload, which removes embedded metadata. Your profile is private by default; only you can see it. If you choose to make it public, anyone can see your username, bio, avatar, join month and your public crags. You can switch it back to private or remove the data at any time in your profile settings.

**Content you submit.** Crag entries (name, location coordinates, description, parking and family notes, guidebook references), uploaded photos with captions, and the username that created them. Uploaded photos are re-encoded on upload, which removes embedded metadata such as location or device information (EXIF). If a crag has no coordinates yet, the location stored in an uploaded photo (except photos in the category "Parking") is read once, before it is removed, and used to fill in the crag coordinates. It is not kept in the photo. The coordinates become part of the crag entry like any coordinates you type in, and you can change or remove them at any time. Coordinates you entered yourself are never replaced. Photos and captions may show or name people. Please do not upload identifiable people without their consent.

**Review conversations.** When you submit a crag for review, you and the moderators can write messages to each other on the crag's page, for example about requested changes. Only you and the moderators see this conversation, together with the review steps (submitted, changes requested, published). It is kept with the crag as its review history. If you delete your account, your name is removed from it.

**Change suggestions.** You can suggest a change to a crag someone else created, with text and optionally photos. Your suggestion, the messages about it and the photos are only visible to you, the crag's creator and the moderators. Photos are re-encoded like all uploads (location data is removed). Photos that are accepted become part of the crag's gallery and are shown with the crag like any other photo; all other suggested photos are deleted when the suggestion is closed. The suggestion and its messages are kept with the crag; if you delete your account, your name is removed from them.

**Notifications.** When someone comments on, rates or adds photos to a crag you created, or when your crag is reviewed, we store a notification for you: what happened, which crag, who did it and when. Only you see your notifications, on the "Notifications" page and as a counter next to your username. In your notification settings you choose for each kind of notification whether you see it in the app and whether you also get it by email; emails about comments, ratings and photos are off unless you switch them on. Opening that page marks them as read. Notifications that are read are deleted after 180 days. All your notifications are deleted with your account, and if the other person deletes their account, their name is removed from your notifications.

**Session data.** After you log in, a session cookie keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary for the login function. We do not use marketing or tracking cookies.

**Visitor counter.** Only if you accept in the cookie banner, and to count how many different people visit per day, we set a cookie that contains only the current date (and a marker if you were logged in) and expires at midnight. It prevents reloads from being counted twice. We store only two daily totals (all visitors, and visitors who were logged in), no IP address, user agent or other visitor data. If you decline, nothing is counted and the cookie is not set. Your choice is remembered in a cookie named `cookie_consent` for 180 days; you can change it at any time via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

**Server logs.** Our hosting and network infrastructure temporarily records technical data such as IP address, time of request, requested page, and browser type. This is needed to operate and secure the site.

**Emails.** We send account-related emails (for example sign-up confirmation or password reset) through the transactional email provider Resend. If you submit a crag, we also email you when it is published or when the reviewers ask you for changes. The email contains the crag name, the outcome, the reviewers' reason (if any) and a link to the crag.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis (GDPR) |
|---|---|---|
| Creating and operating your account | Account data, session data | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Publishing and managing crag entries and photos | Content, username | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Sending account emails and review notifications | Email address, crag name, review outcome | Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR |
| Operating and securing the server | Server logs | Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a stable and secure service) |
| Complying with legal obligations | As required | Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR |

## 4. Service providers and recipients

We use the following processors. Data is passed on only to the extent needed for their service.

- **Hosting:** The server is a homeserver.
- **Cloudflare** (Cloudflare Tunnel): routes traffic to the server and may process connection data as a proxy.
- **Resend** (transactional email): processes email addresses and message content for delivery.

Where a provider processes data outside the EU/EEA, we rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

**External links.** Some pages link to Google Maps. Google receives data only when you actively click the link, and the Google privacy policy applies from that point on.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Retention

- Account data: until you delete your account or request deletion.
- Crag entries and photos: as long as they remain published. When you delete your account, your username is removed from the entries and photos you created. Crag information that others rely on (such as location and approach details) stays published without any link to you. Personal data in them, such as photos showing people or captions naming people, is removed on request. See section 5 of our [Terms of Use](/docs/terms/).
- Notifications: 180 days after you have read them, or until you delete your account.
- Review conversations: as long as the crag exists; your name is removed when you delete your account.
- Change suggestions: the text and messages as long as the crag exists (your name is removed when you delete your account); suggested photos until the suggestion is closed, unless they are accepted into the gallery.
- Server logs: only for as long as needed for operation and security, then deleted.

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your personal data (Art. 15)
- have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16)
- have data deleted (Art. 17)
- restrict processing (Art. 18)
- data portability (Art. 20)
- object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21)
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (Art. 7(3))

To exercise these rights, email impressum@gr8base.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. For Jena, Germany, this is the Thüringer Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (TLDI), Häßlerstraße 8, 99096 Erfurt, [tlfdi.de](https://www.tlfdi.de).

## 7. Children

The site is intended for adults who manage their own accounts. Children may view public crag information, but should not create accounts or upload personal data without a parent or guardian.

## 8. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords, CSRF protection, and regular updates of the software we run. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
