# What is gr8base, and what is it not?

## Who is it for?

Climbers for whom the base and the approach decide where to go: parents with children of different ages, kids' groups, or anyone who does enjoy a nice base with space to relax.

## What do I get?

| What gr8base has | What it means for you |
|---|---|
| Family ratings for babies, ages 2-4 and ages 5+ | You see quickly whether a crag suits your children, without reading a whole description |
| Approach time and features (rockfall, fall hazard, stroller-friendly) and the same for the base area | You know what to expect before you load the car, and "none" means someone checked |
| Photos of the base, the approach and the crag | A few pictures tell you more about the rock, the ground and the conditions than words or a topo |
| Every crag is checked by reviewers before it is published | You can trust the entries more than anonymous listings |
| Ratings, comments and *Suggest a change* from the community | Entries improve with the experience of the people who were there |
| Installable on your phone, with optional notifications | The crag information is at hand when you stand in the parking lot |

## What gr8base is not

**Not in scope**

- A guidebook. It does not want to become one: no route topos, no grades, no route descriptions. It points to the guidebooks that cover a crag.
- A native app. The website can be installed on your phone, that is all.
- Trip planning or booking: no accommodation, no partner finding, no events.
